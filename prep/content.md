##### Software
*The first app I found was sketchpad
* basic teaching of how to draw
* Lots of things given to u such as different brushes and more
* Gives you assisted tools to better draw
* Allows people with color blindness to draw as well
 * https://sketch.io/sketchpad/

*The second app I found was KRESKA
* Allows you to have a reference on the side
* Allows for different fonts and different background style
* Helps with animations
* gives drawing lessons
* Hints on how to improve a drawing
* https://kreska.art/

* The third app I found was Krita
* This app was made for people who wants to show their art to a wider audience
* Helps artist make money off art
* Allows for artist to show their art to a wider audeince
* It is also able to help them animate for different companies
* https://krita.org/en/
